Salamander Grand Piano V3
======================

Piano samples in this folder are from the Salamander Grand Piano V3
by Alexander Holm.

Source: https://archive.org/details/SalamanderGrandPianoV3
Sample distribution: https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/

Licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0):
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

Files here are a reduced set prepared for the web instrument: one note
every three semitones (A0 to C8), taken from the mid-velocity layer
(v8) of the original set, with the inaudible tail trimmed and a short
fade added at the end. Each file is a 96 kbps mono MP3 at 44.1 kHz.
The audio is otherwise unmodified; gain, velocity filtering, stereo
panning, reverb and compression are applied at runtime by app.js.
